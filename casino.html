How Limbo Works

Choose your bet and target multiplier. The system generates a random crash point using cryptographic RNG. If the crash point meets or exceeds your target, you win your bet × multiplier. Otherwise, you lose.

How Cases Work

Each case contains a pool of items with different rarities and values. When you open a case, our cryptographic RNG selects one item based on predefined probabilities. The animation is just for show – your result is determined instantly.

Under the Hood: Each item has probability pi and value vi. Expected value: EV = Σ(pi × vi). Case prices are set so EV ≈ price × RTP (typically 85-92%), creating a house edge. Higher-tier cases have better items but similar return rates.

How Blackjack Works

Classic 21 game. You and the dealer receive cards from a shuffled deck. Number cards = face value, face cards = 10, Aces = 1 or 11. Goal: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Blackjack (Ace + 10-card) pays 3:2.

Rules: Hit = take card, Stand = stop, Double = double bet + one card. Dealer must hit until 17+. You bust = instant loss. Natural blackjack beats 21. Push on ties.

House Edge: ~0.5% with optimal play. This game uses 6 decks, reshuffled after each hand.
